What you can do at the retailer counter

Same services as a small bank counter. Run by someone the customer already knows.

Send money, pay bills, recharge, give cash, keep a wallet, and help with cards. You cannot go below zero. Every rupee is recorded.

01

Send money to any bank account

Customer gives you cash. You send it to their family by IMPS or NEFT. Limit is ₹5,000 per send and ₹25,000 in a month. You need their mobile, ID, and an OTP.

How it works

02

Give cash with Aadhaar

Customer puts thumb on the machine. Money comes from their bank. You give cash from the retailer counter. We only keep last 4 digits of Aadhaar — never the fingerprint.

How it works

03

UPI to cash

Show a QR. Customer pays from PhonePe or GPay and enters UPI PIN. After it shows paid, give them cash. Maximum ₹5,000 at a time.

How it works

04

Your retailer wallet

Add money first, then do business. You cannot send more than the balance. If a transfer fails, the money comes back to your wallet.

How it works

05

Pay electricity and other bills

Customer brings a CA number or a policy number. You fetch the bill, take cash, and pay from your wallet. Electricity, water, gas, broadband, LPG, credit card, loan, education, and municipal — all on BBPS.

How it works

06

Mobile and DTH recharge

Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL, Tata Play, Dish TV. Pick a plan or type an amount. The wallet is debited before the operator is credited.

How it works

07

FASTag recharge

Enter the vehicle number. Some banks show the outstanding first. You pay from the wallet and hand over the receipt.

How it works

08

Collect an LIC premium

Fetch the premium on the policy number, then pay. Same wallet hold as a bill. Commission posts when it succeeds.

How it works

09

Help with an FD credit card

You collect ID and PAN. Customer puts money in a fixed deposit from their own phone. You never take that cash at the counter. The retailer earns a fee when the card is issued.

How it works

010

Retailers, distributors, and admin

Admin appoints distributors. Distributors add retailers. There is no public signup. Commission is split when a transfer succeeds.

How it works

A walk-in customer

Four steps. A few minutes.

  1. 1

    Customer comes in

    Cash in hand, or a UPI app open. They do not need a gfinpay account.

  2. 2

    You take details

    Mobile and ID for sending money. Name and mobile for cash. QR for UPI.

  3. 3

    Wallet is locked

    The amount is held in your wallet before the bank is called.

  4. 4

    Done

    When the bank confirms, you get a receipt. Your commission is added the same time.

How money is kept

Every rupee is written in a passbook that cannot be edited.

You add money to the wallet first. When you send, that amount is held. If the bank succeeds, it is taken. If it fails, it comes back. Commission is split when the transfer is successful.

Customers do not get a gfinpay wallet. Their money stays in their own bank.

Who uses gfinpay.

Retailer

You run the counter.

Phone on the table. Wallet on top. Send money, bills, recharge, cash, and cards — next to oil and SIM cards.

Distributor

You look after the area.

See retailers, balances, and your commission. You do not handle customer cash.

Bank / partner

You hold the licence.

You keep accounts and cards. We keep retailers, wallets, and a daily file you can match.

What we will not do

  • Hold customer deposits or run a public app for everyone.
  • Let a retailer send money on credit — wallet must have balance first.
  • Let you collect FD cash at the counter.
  • Allow anyone to sign up. Retailers are appointed.

What an auditor will find

  • Monthly send-money limits locked so two sends cannot overshoot.
  • Aadhaar last-4 only. No fingerprint stored.
  • PAN last-4 only on the till.
  • Day-end files that match the bank, not a screenshot.
